    Power rankings

      Arrows compare each player's rank with the last closed week. Ratings use Elo: everyone starts at 1000 and each result moves both players by the same amount. Beating a stronger, higher-rated opponent earns many more points (and costs them more); beating a weaker one earns few. Swiss events still score 3 for a win, 1 for a tie. Sprites beside a name show the deck that player used most recently.

                      Card database

                      Cards come from TCGdex, a free card database that needs no account or key. The first import fetches cards one by one and can take 10 to 20 minutes, newest sets first. Keep this tab open. After that, Update cards only fetches new or changed sets and takes seconds. Use Refresh everything after a Standard rotation. Card pictures load from TCGdex and are not stored.
